India Government Documents & Certificates
 Database
PRD / Master Document — 28 States + 8 Union Territories + Central Government
Purpose: create a comprehensive website/database containing important government documents and certificates
only. The source material identifies core documents, state-wise documents, Union Territories, and Central
Government documents.

6. Website Structure
Home → All Documents → Central Documents → States & UTs → Document Categories → Search. Each document
page should show its name, alternate names, category, government level, state/UT, description, importance, common
uses, applicable users, issuing authority, official source and last verified date.
7. Accuracy Notice
The source material explicitly cautions that documents should not be labelled universally mandatory: whether a
document is required depends on the service, applicant, state and purpose. Government information should be
verified through the relevant official authority.

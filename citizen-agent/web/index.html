1. Scope of Digital Assistance Platform

Citizen-Service Agent ("CivicAgent") is an automated digital public assistance platform designed to facilitate citizen interactions with state and central government public service guarantee portals across India (including e-District, RTPS, and Aaple Sarkar systems). The platform assists citizens with discovering eligible schemes, validating prerequisite documents using OCR, generating pre-filled applications, and tracking processing timelines.

2. Strictly ₹0 Platform Fee & Complete Fee Transparency

The platform strictly levies ₹0 in assistance or platform fees. Any statutory fee charged is mandated exclusively by the relevant government department (e.g. ₹0 for Income Certificate, or statutory fees for commercial licenses). The platform strictly forbids unofficial middleman payments, facilitation bribes, or informal processing fees.

3. 24x7 Round-The-Clock Citizen Guidance Helpline

To ensure all citizens, regardless of digital literacy, can access government entitlements, the platform operates a continuous 24x7 Toll-Free Citizen Helpline: 1800-180-2026.

  • Helpline Number: 1800-180-2026 (Toll-free across all telecom networks in India).
  • Operating Hours: 24 Hours a Day, 7 Days a Week, 365 Days a Year.
  • Vernacular Support: Guidance available in Hindi, English, Punjabi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and Kannada.
  • Assistance Scope: Guidance on required certificates, document formats, application status queries, and grievance lodging.

4. Citizen Obligations & Authenticity of Information

Citizens represent and warrant that all personal data, identity numbers, and supporting documents provided during the application process are accurate, lawful, and authentic. Submission of forged, altered, or fraudulent identity proofs is strictly prohibited and subject to prosecution under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

5. Data Protection, Privacy & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

All personal information is processed strictly under the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP 2023):

  • Explicit Consent Gating: No data or document is shared without affirmative, purpose-limited citizen consent.
  • Aadhaar Masking: Aadhaar numbers are masked by default (showing only the last 4 digits) to protect citizen privacy.
  • Cryptographic Hash Integrity: Every document verification event is recorded in a tamper-evident SHA-256 audit log.
  • Right to Erasure: Citizens retain the legal right to request deletion of temporary OCR files and uploaded previews.

6. Sovereign Government Authority & Department Decisions

The platform functions as an automated digital facilitation interface. The ultimate statutory authority to approve, query, or reject any application rests entirely with designated government officers (such as the Sub-Divisional Officer, Circle Officer, or District Magistrate). The platform does not alter statutory rules or bypass departmental verification.

7. Audit Trail & Service Reliability

All application steps, payment verifications, and status changes are permanently logged with immutable timestamps. While the platform guarantees 99.9% uptime, processing delays attributable to state departmental servers or field inquiries are subject to official government turnaround times.

8. Grievance Redressal & Contact Information

For grievances, appeals, or legal inquiries, citizens may contact our designated Grievance Cell:
